The door

This is the other side of “Present my score”: a demo of what a website, bar door or Discord bot would run. You set a minimum score, it shows a request code, the person uses it on their phone and shows you a token, and this page says ACCEPT or DENY.

Set this door's policy

“Audience” is just the name of this door — the token is locked to it so it cannot be replayed elsewhere. “Minimum score” is your policy: the person is accepted when their score is at or above it. 70 is a high bar; 30 admits anyone verified in person at least once.

What does the door learn about the person?

Only what is inside the token: score, status, assurance tier, cluster count and a pseudonymous Trust ID. It never sees the attestation graph — who verified them — and it never sees an ID document. “Token valid” means the cryptography checks out; “Decision” is this door's own policy on top of that.
